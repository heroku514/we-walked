We Walked keeps one week of walks on this phone. Today starts open. Tap We walked when the family goes out. The six days before today stay here too.

Tomorrow can be opened, and it waits. A walk cannot be marked until that day arrives. Clear the week when you want to begin again.

We Walked does not use an account, ads, a camera, or a network connection. The days in the app come from the phone's calendar.
